Before we pray, let's first take a moment to listen to God's word and then we'll pray together. Jeremiah 29:13 says, "You will seek me and find me when you seek me with all your heart."

Father in heaven, your word is pure and true, and this promise from Jeremiah burns deep within my soul. You have said that if I seek you with all my heart, I will find you—not perhaps, not maybe, but with certainty. Today I come before you with a longing that I cannot satisfy on my own. My heart aches to know you more—not just to know about you, but to truly walk in your presence, to breathe in your nearness, and to live each moment as one who has found the treasure of life in you.

Lord, I confess that too often my seeking has been half-hearted. Forgive me for the times when I have sought answers more than I sought the Answerer, when I have chased blessings more than I chased the One who blesses. I repent of shallow devotion and ask for a hunger that will not be satisfied until I have found you in deeper ways. You have said that those who hunger and thirst for righteousness will be filled (Matthew 5:6). I do not want to remain at the edges of your presence; I want to step fully into the Holy of Holies, where the flame of first love burns brightly.

Strip away the false gods in my life—the idols of comfort, recognition, control, and self-sufficiency. What does "all my heart" truly mean? It means no divided loyalties, no hidden corners kept from you. Help me to love you with all my heart, soul, mind, and strength (Mark 12:30)—not just with emotions, but with obedience in both sacred and ordinary moments.

Seeking you is active, like a traveler searching for hidden treasure, a shepherd searching for a lost sheep, or a woman turning her house upside down for a missing coin (Luke 15:8). Give me the perseverance of Jacob who wrestled with the angel and said, "I will not let you go unless you bless me" (Genesis 32:26). You have promised that if I draw near to you, you will draw near to me (James 4:8). Search me and know me (Psalm 139:23-24).

Father, I seek you not merely for what you can give me, but for who you are—because in your presence there is fullness of joy, and at your right hand are pleasures forevermore (Psalm 16:11). Teach me to seek you in the Scriptures for revelation, in prayer by lingering in communion, in worship for the joy of your presence, and in service to reflect your heart. Your presence refines, purifies, heals, strengthens, and transforms me into the likeness of Jesus.

You are not a distant God playing hide-and-seek; you are a Father who runs to meet the prodigal on the road home. I will seek you in the morning before the day's noise begins, and in the evening when the shadows fall, until faith becomes sight and I see you face to face. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.
