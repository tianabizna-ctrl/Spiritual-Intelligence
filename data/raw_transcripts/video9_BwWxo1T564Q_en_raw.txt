Let me speak over someone: you have things, but you lack men. I pray for you—men that include helpers, comforters, financiers, and prophetic people to speak: this week, may God bring quality men to show up in your life! Everyone here appointed unto death, in the name of Jesus the Son of the Living God, by reason of this prayer we avert death from your life and from your family! These Egyptians you see today, you will see them no more forever (Exodus 14:13)!

The blessing is conferred upon people by saying—words mixed with faith:
1. Covenant of Plenty (Deuteronomy 30:9): "And the Lord thy God will make thee plenteous in every work of thine hand, in the fruit of thy body, and in the fruit of thy cattle, and in the fruit of thy land, for good: for the Lord will again rejoice over thee for good." I prophesy: let plenty locate you in this season; I drive scarcity from you!
2. Exemption from Sickness (Deuteronomy 7:15): "And the Lord will take away from thee all sickness, and will put none of the evil diseases of Egypt, which thou knowest, upon thee." For you and your loved ones, be free from sickness and infirmity!
3. Victory Over Every Weapon & Tongue (Isaiah 54:17): "No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn."
4. Accuracy of Direction & Unfailing Supply (Isaiah 58:11): "And the Lord shall guide thee continually, and satisfy thy soul in drought, and make fat thy bones: and thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a spring of water, whose waters fail not." Every confusion and misdirection ends now; receive accuracy of direction and unfailing supply!
5. AVisible Sign of Favor to End Shame (Psalm 86:17): "Shew me a token [sign] for good; that they which hate me may see it, and be ashamed: because thou, Lord, hast holpen me, and comforted me." Just as Elisha parted the Jordan as a sign that the spirit of Elijah rested upon him, may God show a visible sign of favor and speed in your life this week!
6. The Gift of Quality Men (Isaiah 43:4): "Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable, and I have loved thee: therefore will I give men for thee, and people for thy life." Just as the man at the pool of Bethesda needed one man, may the destiny helper sent by God gravitate toward your destiny now!
7. The Priestly Blessing: Kept, Favored & Given Peace (Numbers 6:24-26, John 17:12, Psalm 44:3): "The Lord bless thee, and keep thee" (engraved in the palm of His hands beyond the reach of curses); "The Lord make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee" (covered completely with the favor of God); "The Lord lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace" (the highest definition of success is peace that surpasses all understanding). Where you have been crawling, begin to fly like the eagles; I tear off every veil covering your visibility!
8. Resurrection of Prayer Life & Spiritual Passion (John 11:25): Everything dead or dying—your prayer life, your Word study life, your passion for the house of God—come back to life now in the name of Jesus!
9. Trusteeship of Kingdom Wealth (Psalm 66:12): God brings you through water and fire into a wealthy place—not for carnal materialism or compromise, but so you become a passionate trustee of the wealth of the Kingdom!
10. Threefold Apostolic Impartation (Luke 2:52, Isaiah 8:18, Numbers 27:20): First, carry the Grace of Favor evidently with God and with men. Second, become a Sign and a Wonder to your generation. Third, receive the Mantle of Honor—for you can respect yourself, but another must confer honor upon you—that your true worth may be acknowledged and rewarded in Jesus' name! Amen.
