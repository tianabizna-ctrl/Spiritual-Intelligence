Father in heaven, I come before you at
the dawn of this new day with a heart
that longs to be near you. As the light
begins to touch the earth, I am reminded
that you are the one who causes the sun
to rise on the just and the unjust, that
every day is birthed in your mercy, and
that even now as I speak, your hand is
sustaining all creation.
I breathe in deeply and remember that my
very breath is a gift from you. That I
woke not by chance but by your will and
that my being alive today is part of a
plan you began before I ever took my
first breath. I do not take that
lightly.
Lord, you have brought me through the
night, keeping me when I could not keep
myself.
I thank you for the rest you provided,
for the unseen protection you gave me,
for the way you guard my soul as a
shepherd watches his sheep.
I rise today, not as one who owns the
day, but as a steward of the hours you
have given me.
Teach me to walk in them wisely, to be
mindful of the moments, so that not one
is wasted in fruitless wandering.
I thank you for the simple blessings
that often pass without my notice: the
strength of my body, the ability to
think and speak, the food that will
nourish me, the water that will refresh
me, the roof that covers me, and the
clothes that warm me.
There are many in this world without
these things, and I am humbled that you
have provided them for me. May my
gratitude never be shallow. May it spill
over into generosity that I may reflect
your kindness to others.
Search me, Lord. Look into my heart and
see if there is any thought or motive
that does not honor you. I confess that
there have been moments when my words
were careless, when my thoughts strayed
into doubt or pride,
when my actions were shaped more by my
own desires than by love for you or
others.
Forgive me for those times I sought my
own comfort over obedience, my own way
over your way.
Wash me clean today. Let no shadow of
sin remain to cloud my fellowship with
you. Make me sensitive to the whispers
of your Spirit, quick to turn from wrong
and quick to embrace what is right. As I
stand on the edge of this day, I place
it entirely in your hands. My plans, my
meetings, my work, my conversations,
all of it is yours.
Guide my steps, Lord, for without you I
stumble.
You have promised in your word that if I
trust in you with all my heart and lean
not on my own understanding and in all
my ways acknowledge you, you will make
my paths straight. Proverbs 3:5-6.
Let this promise shape my outlook. May I
walk not by sight but by faith,
believing that even in uncertainty, your
wisdom is leading me.
Grant me strength for the tasks ahead.
Help me to approach my responsibilities
not as burdens but as opportunities to
serve you.
Guard me from the temptation to rush
through the day, missing the gentle ways
you are present.
Keep my eyes open to moments where I can
be a light in someone's darkness, an
encourager to the weary, a servant to
those in need.
Let me carry your presence into every
room I enter, so that even without
words, others might sense the peace and
hope that only comes from you. I ask for
wisdom, Lord, the kind that does not
come from books or from human
cleverness, but from your Spirit.
Help me to discern what matters most, to
know when to speak and when to remain
silent, when to press forward and when
to wait. Let my mind be steady and
clear, free from fear and confusion.
Remind me that you have not given me a
spirit of fear, but of power, love, and
a sound mind. Father, I lift before you
those I love: family, friends, and all
who cross my path. Protect them today.
Surround them with your favor as with a
shield. For those who are ill, bring
healing. For those who are weighed down
by grief, bring comfort. For those who
are far from you, draw them back with
cords of kindness.
For those who are making decisions,
grant them clarity and courage to choose
what is right. I pray also for those I
may never meet but who are in need of
your hand today: the poor, the hungry,
the oppressed, the lonely, the
brokenhearted.
Send help to them, Lord. Send laborers
into the fields to bring relief, hope,
and the good news of your kingdom.
If there is some way I can be part of
the answer to these prayers, make me
willing and ready to obey. Keep me
mindful of the eternal in the midst of
the temporary.
It is easy to become entangled in the
little frustrations of life, to let
small inconveniences steal joy.
Teach me to rise above such things, to
see the bigger picture of what you are
doing in and through me.
Remind me often that my citizenship is
in heaven, that my hope is not in
circumstances, but in Christ alone.
Help me to walk in humility, not
thinking of myself more highly than I
ought, but esteeming others above
myself. Let my love be genuine, not
shallow or self-serving.
Let my service be cheerful, not
grudging. Let my words be seasoned with
grace, bringing life and not harm.
Let my reactions reflect patience and
gentleness even when tested. When I grow
weary, be my strength. When I feel
discouraged, be my hope. When I am
uncertain, be my anchor. When I am
tempted, be my escape. When I am lonely,
remind me of your presence. And when I
succeed, keep me humble and quick to
give you the glory.
As the hours unfold, give me a spirit of
prayer that does not fade after this
moment, but lingers throughout the day.
A heart that speaks with you in the
quiet of my thoughts, a readiness to
lift thanks and joy, and to call on you
in need.
Teach me to abide in you so closely that
your desires become my desires, your
heart my heart, your ways my ways. For
you have said, "I am the vine. You are
the branches. If you remain in me and I
in you, you will bear much fruit. Apart
from me, you can do nothing." John 15:5.
Let that truth be my compass. And
finally, Lord, I pray for a deeper
hunger for you.
Let me not be satisfied with a shallow
faith or a passing knowledge of you.
Draw me into the depths of your word,
into the richness of communion with you
in prayer, into the joy of obedience,
into the peace of surrender.
Let me know you more tomorrow than I do
today and more today than I did
yesterday. So here I am, Lord, yours
completely. Take my mind, my heart, my
hands, my voice. Use me to bless, to
serve, to speak truth, to bring comfort,
to extend grace. Keep me close. Keep me
faithful. Keep me in step with your
Spirit.
And at the close of this day, may I look
back and see your fingerprints on every
moment, knowing I walked not alone, but
with you.
I offer this prayer in the name of
Jesus, my savior, my lord, my friend, my
life. Amen.
